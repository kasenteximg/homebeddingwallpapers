Kasentex Bedding Wallpapers ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection and use for our Android mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use
We believe in absolute privacy. Kasentex Bedding Wallpapers does not personally collect, store, use, transmit, or share any personal information or data of any kind.

No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user profiles.

2. Device Storage & Wallpaper Settings
The app requires standard Android permissions to access your device's wallpaper settings and local storage. This access is strictly used for the technical functionality of downloading, saving, and applying the bedding wallpapers directly to your screen. This process happens entirely on your device, and we have no access to it.

3. Third-Party Hosting (GitHub)
To provide you with high-quality wallpapers without making the app's file size too large, the images are hosted remotely in a public GitHub repository.

When the app downloads a wallpaper, your device connects directly to GitHub's servers. Like virtually all internet traffic, GitHub automatically receives and logs standard technical data required to fulfill the request, such as your IP address and device user-agent.

This data is handled entirely by GitHub in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not have access to GitHub's server logs, nor do we track which wallpapers you download.

4. Children's Privacy
Because we do not personally collect any data from our users, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
